I am Xiong Shi (石雄), a global change ecologist. I received my bachelor's degree from Shaanxi Normal University and my master's degree from Xishuangbanna Tropical Botanical Garden, Chinese Academy of Sciences. I then earned my PhD at the University of Konstanz, Germany, under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Mark van Kleunen. Since May 2026, I have been a postdoctoral researcher in Prof. Dr. Jingyun Fang's lab at Peking University, where I continue to investigate how global change shapes the coexistence of native and non-native plants.
